      What is private

      • Confidential from observers: each contribution amount and the total raised. A successful settlement only shows that the total reached the target.
      • Public: campaign terms, each contributor's Solana address, the number of contributions, which instruction ran and the outcome.
      • Known to the coordinator: every contribution amount; it must hold every preimage to settle. Nobody can contribute unless it is online and co-signs, so it can delay or refuse contributions, and if it never settles the campaign fails and everyone refunds, even when the target was met. It cannot move funds anywhere but the beneficiary or back to contributors.
      • Known to the beneficiary: the total it receives.
      • Not hidden from the prover: SPP proofs are built by the local prover server, which receives the full private transaction.
